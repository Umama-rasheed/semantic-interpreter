What is Semantic Interpretation?

Semantic Interpretation is the core NLP task of translating a standard English sentence into a structured, machine-readable format. Rather than just scanning individual words, the system analyzes the grammatical structure to identify the core semantic roles:

  • • Predicate: The core action or verb (Action)
  • • Agent: Who performed the action (Subject)
  • • Patient: What was affected (Object)
  • • Context: Where and when it happened

Finally, it dynamically assembles these extracted components into a formal logical representation:

Predicate(Agent, Patient, Place, Time)

The Pipeline Explained

1.

Input: The user enters a raw English sentence into the application.

2.

Tokenize: Punctuation is stripped, and the sentence is split into an array of individual words.

3.

Rule Match: The engine scans for a verb in the dictionary and checks for passive voice markers ("was", "by").

4.

Extract: Surrounding words are classified. The noun before the verb becomes the Subject; nouns after become Objects, Places, or Time.

5.

Representation: The extracted components are dynamically formatted into a final logic predicate formula.
